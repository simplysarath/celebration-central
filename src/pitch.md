Celebration Central — Pitch
Celebration Central is a native iOS app that turns our grocery stores into a one-stop party planning destination. Instead of customers bouncing between cakes.com, the deli counter, and floral — they tell us the occasion, guest count, and budget, and our AI concierge instantly assembles a complete celebration package spanning Bakery (custom cakes via Decopac, cupcakes, cookie trays), Deli (sandwich pinwheels, cheese & meat platters, wing trays), Floral (centerpieces, bouquets), and party essentials (balloons, plates, drinks). The experience is visual and tap-driven — no chat interface — powered by a LangGraph agent that dynamically decides what to show next. Each department section surfaces a smart recommendation with a carousel of alternatives, filters, and a text field for special requests like "Write Happy Birthday Emma" or "No nuts." Customers customize, checkout, and schedule delivery or pickup in one seamless flow. The app plugs directly into our existing eCommerce REST APIs — zero new infrastructure — and is designed to dramatically increase average order value by cross-selling across departments that today operate in silos.
